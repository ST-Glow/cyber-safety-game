# Noto Sans SC font source

- Package: `@fontsource/noto-sans-sc@5.3.0`
- Upstream family: Noto Sans SC
- Source repository: https://github.com/google/fonts
- Package registry: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/noto-sans-sc
- Included file: merged Simplified Chinese and Latin subsets, weight 600, TTF
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `OFL.txt`

The two Fontsource WOFF2 subsets were converted to TTF and merged with FontTools 4.63.0. The resulting local TTF lets native and Web builds render Chinese without relying on a system font or an online CDN at runtime.
